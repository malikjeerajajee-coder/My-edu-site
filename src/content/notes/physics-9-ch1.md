## Physical Quantities

A **physical quantity** is anything that can be measured.

## Base vs Derived

| Type | Example | Unit |
|------|---------|------|
| Base | Length | metre |
| Base | Mass | kilogram |
| Derived | Speed | m/s |
| Derived | Force | newton |

## Prefixes

- **kilo (k)** = 10³
- **mega (M)** = 10⁶
- **milli (m)** = 10⁻³
